# PIGI — Pill Reminder for iPhone

This is a product information page for PIGI, a pill reminder and medication reminder for iPhone (iOS), published by Anna Stakhiv. The app is proprietary; this page does not publish its source code.

[Official website](https://pigi-support.arsenstahiv.chatgpt.site/) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pigi-pill-reminder/id6810865766) · [Support](https://pigi-support.arsenstahiv.chatgpt.site/support/) · [Privacy](https://pigi-support.arsenstahiv.chatgpt.site/privacy/)

Enter medicines, doses and schedules, then mark doses as taken. PIGI supports snooze choices of 1, 2 or 3 hours in the app, remaining medicine tracking, separate profiles on one iPhone, and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Medicine names can be hidden in main lists, notifications and widgets; editing forms and reports still show the names.

Medicine records stay on the device. No PIGI account is required. Download and features are free, with optional one-time tips through Apple and no subscription. Requires iOS 17 or later.

![Medicine list with illustrative records](https://pigi-support.arsenstahiv.chatgpt.site/images/medicines.png)

Stock tracking is based on entered quantities and doses marked taken; there is no separate refill alert. Profiles do not provide remote caregiver monitoring or cross-device synchronization. Reopen the app before the reminder-renewal date shown in the app. Reminder delivery depends on notification permission and iOS settings.

PIGI organizes reminders and records; it does not diagnose conditions or recommend treatment.
